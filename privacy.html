Slipstream

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Slipstream does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers belonging to us. The app makes no network connections other than to your own game console on your own local network, and everything it records stays on your device.

What the app does

Slipstream reads the telemetry that your PlayStation broadcasts while you drive, over your local network, and draws it on screen. That telemetry describes the car — speed, position on track, tire temperatures, fuel and so on. It is received directly from your console, used to draw the display, and never sent anywhere else.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. None of it is transmitted to us or to any third party.

WhatWhy
The local network address of your console So the app can find it again next time. You type it in; it is a private address on your own network.
Display preferences To keep the app set up the way you left it.
Reference laps and stint history To compare your current lap against your previous driving. Lap times and positions on track, nothing about you.
A per-day count of laps used on the free tier To apply the free tier's daily allowance. A date and a number.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. This data may also be included in backups you choose to make of your device (for example iCloud or Finder backups). Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

Local network access

iOS asks your permission for Slipstream to access devices on your local network. The app needs it to receive telemetry from your console, which is the app's entire purpose. Traffic stays on your network. If you decline, the live display cannot work, but Replay mode still does.

Subscriptions and payment

Slipstream offers an optional auto-renewing subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see and never receive your name, email address, payment-card details, or billing address. The app asks the App Store, on your device, only whether an active subscription exists, so it knows which features to unlock. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage telemetry of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, in the App Store sense or any other. Nothing about you is shared with anyone, so there is nothing to link across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No crash or diagnostic reporting to us. If you choose to share diagnostics with Apple, that is a setting in iOS and is between you and Apple.

Children

Slipstream is not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above. If the app ever begins collecting data — it does not today — that change will be described here before it takes effect.
