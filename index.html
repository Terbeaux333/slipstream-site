A racing telemetry display for iPhone and iPad.

Slipstream shows what your car is doing while you drive — your speed against your own reference lap, the road ahead, tire temperatures, fuel in laps rather than litres, and where the time went, corner by corner.

Getting started

  1. Put your iPhone or iPad and your console on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Within your PS5, enter Settings -> Network -> Connection Status -> View Connection Status -> IP Address (IPv4)
  3. Open Slipstream, enter that address, and tap Connect.
  4. Allow the local-network prompt when iOS asks. The app needs it to receive telemetry, and without it the live display cannot work.

Trying it without a console

Slipstream ships with a recorded lap. Choose Replay to watch the whole display work — the speed tape, the zone strip, the tires and the fuel gauge — with no console and no network at all. It is the quickest way to see what the app does, and it is also how to check the app is working if live telemetry is not arriving.

Common problems

Nothing arrives in live mode

  • Check both devices are on the same network, and that it is not a guest network — those often block devices from seeing each other.
  • Check the IP address. It changes if your router reassigns it.
  • Check the local-network permission is allowed, in Settings → Slipstream.
  • Confirm the app itself is fine by loading Replay.

The track is wrong, or the app says it does not know the track

The app recognises a circuit from your position on it. A track it has no profile for is reported honestly rather than guessed at, since a confidently wrong track is worse than none.

A subscription question

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To view, change or cancel one, open your Apple Account subscriptions. Restoring a purchase on a new device is the Restore Purchases button on the subscription screen. We cannot see or alter your subscription, and refunds go through Apple.
